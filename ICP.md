# LeadTube ICP

This is the ONE file that describes who you're looking for. Every search,
fit score, filter and email check reads it. Change it here and nothing else.

How to edit
- Lines starting with ">" are explanations and are ignored.
- Lists: separate items with commas or new lines. Lower or upper case is the same.
- `*` means "any ending": `facilitat*` matches facilitator, facilitation...
- `{a|b}` makes one item per option: `{executive|ceo} coach` = executive coach, ceo coach.
- Don't rename the section titles (the numbers can stay as they are).
- Restart searches after editing; the dashboard picks changes up on the next run.
